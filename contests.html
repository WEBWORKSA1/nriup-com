Mera UP — tell the world your UP story

For everyone whose story runs through Uttar Pradesh: NRIs, OCIs, Gulf workers and Girmitiya descendants from Fiji to Trinidad. Entries close in ….

Roots Story

600–1,500 words about an ancestor, a village or a journey. English or Hindi.

📷

Photo of UP

One original photograph taken in UP, with a 50-word caption.

🎬

60-Second Film

“UP from abroad” — a vertical or horizontal film up to 60 seconds.

🤝

Pravasi Hero

Nominate someone abroad who quietly helps others from UP.

Prizes

Each track has a winner and two runners-up. Winners receive a cash prize and a feature on nriUP; all shortlisted entrants receive a certificate. Cash prize amounts depend on sponsorship and will be posted here before entries close; the target pool is US$1,000 across all tracks. Sponsor a track.

Judging

Originality 40% · Connection to UP 30% · Craft 30%. A panel of three judges reviews entries without names attached. The People's Choice award is decided by public vote, limited to one vote per person per track.

Official rules (summary)

  1. Free entry. No purchase or payment is needed to enter or win. Supporting nriUP does not improve your chances.
  2. Eligibility. Open to individuals aged 18 or over, anywhere in the world, except where prohibited by local law. nriUP staff, judges and their immediate families can't win.
  3. Skill-based. Winners are chosen by judges on the published criteria, not by chance.
  4. Limits. Up to two entries per person per track. Entries must be original and not previously awarded.
  5. Rights. You keep copyright. You grant nriUP a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display your entry on nriUP and its social channels with credit, for the contest and up to two years after.
  6. Content. No hateful, defamatory or sexual content; no content that identifies a private person without consent; no political campaigning.
  7. Prizes. Paid by bank transfer or PayPal within 60 days of the announcement. Winners are responsible for any taxes. If sponsorship falls short, nriUP will award at least certificates and features, and will say so on this page before entries close.
  8. Changes. nriUP may extend dates or cancel a track if there are too few entries, with notice on this page.
  9. Privacy. Entry data is handled under our Privacy Policy.
