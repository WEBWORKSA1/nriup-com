Investing in UP without getting burned

New airports and expressways have made UP a growth story. Growth stories attract sharp sellers. Here's how to tell the opportunity from the pitch.

What you can invest in

  • Residential and commercial property — allowed for NRIs and OCIs without RBI approval. Agricultural land, plantations and farmhouses are not.
  • Authority plots — development authorities such as YEIDA (Yamuna Expressway), Noida, Greater Noida and LDA run plot schemes; read each brochure's eligibility clause for NRIs.
  • Shares, funds and businesses — through NRE (repatriable) or NRO (non-repatriable) routes, subject to India's foreign-investment rules.

Due diligence that takes an afternoon

  1. Search the project on UP RERA — registration, promoter, timelines and complaints.
  2. Check the land on UP Bhulekh and the encumbrance on IGRS UP.
  3. Confirm authority approvals — the map, the lease and the allotment are what make a plot real.
  4. Pay only to the developer's or authority's account, through banking channels, against receipts.
Red flags: “assured returns”, “farmland near the airport” sold to NRIs, cash discounts, a project missing from RERA, or pressure to pay before you see the title papers.

Starting a business

Invest UP is the state's investment promotion agency: sector policies, land bank, events and an investment-intent form. Nivesh Mitra is the single window for licences, NOCs and incentives, with online tracking — the portal reports about 1.49 million licences and NOCs issued. Plan for company registration, GST, local approvals and a resident director where the law requires one.

The Jewar effect

The Yamuna Expressway region around Noida International Airport also carries plans for a film city and logistics parks, according to YEIDA. Infrastructure can lift values over years, but prices along the corridor already reflect much of the story. Buy what you'd be happy to hold for a decade. Read the Jewar guide.
