Visit & pilgrimage

Coming home to UP

More airports, faster expressways and a pilgrimage circuit that now draws visitors from every continent. Plan around the crowds, not into them.

Getting in

Lucknow and Varanasi have long been UP's main international gateways. The big change is Noida International Airport at Jewar: the Prime Minister inaugurated it on 28 March 2026 and commercial flights began on 15 June 2026. Phase one has one runway and a terminal sized for 12 million passengers a year; international services were expected to follow by the end of 2026. Ayodhya and Kushinagar also have airports, useful for pilgrimage trips. Read the Jewar airport guide.

The pilgrimage circuit

  • Ayodhya — the Shri Ram Janmabhoomi temple. Note for NRIs: the temple trust's site says it is not accepting donations in foreign currency at the moment.
  • Varanasi (Kashi) — the Shri Kashi Vishwanath temple offers paid “Sugam Darshan” with an escort, a free live darshan stream and online donations, including for devotees abroad.
  • Prayagraj — the Sangam hosts the Magh Mela every winter; the last Maha Kumbh ran from 13 January to 26 February 2025.
  • Mathura–Vrindavan — the Braj circuit, busiest around Janmashtami and Holi.
  • Sarnath and Kushinagar — key sites of the Buddhist circuit.

Diaspora dates

  • Pravasi Bharatiya Divas 2027 — the 19th convention, organised by the Ministry of External Affairs, is set for 7–9 January 2027 in Kolkata.
  • Uttar Pradesh Diwas — 24 January, the state's foundation day.

Papers for the trip

Indian passport holders need nothing extra. OCI cardholders travel visa-free on their OCI card with their foreign passport. Other foreign-passport holders of Indian origin need a visa or e-visa. If you plan to deal with property or a bank while you're there, carry PAN, passport copies and any POA originals.

Want the trip to also fix a property or paperwork problem? Tell us before you fly; most offices need appointments.
