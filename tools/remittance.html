How to read the result

The mid-market rate is the midpoint between what banks pay to buy and sell a currency. No consumer gets it exactly. A provider that advertises “zero fees” usually earns its money by giving you a lower rate. The true cost is the difference between what your family would receive at the mid-market rate and what they actually receive.

Three habits save the most: compare at least two providers on the day you send, send larger amounts less often when fixed fees apply, and check the rate shown after any first-transfer promotion ends. Read the full guide to sending money to India.
