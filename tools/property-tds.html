Selling property as an NRI? See the TDS first.

Without a certificate, the buyer withholds tax on your whole sale price. On a long-held flat that can be several times the tax you actually owe.

More than 24 months counts as long-term. Inherited property counts from when the previous owner bought it.

The rules behind the numbers

  • For sales on or after 23 July 2024, long-term gains are taxed at 12.5% without indexation, plus surcharge and 4% cess.
  • From tax year 2026-27 the withholding rule sits in section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (it was section 195). The buyer must deduct on every sale by a non-resident — there is no ₹50 lakh threshold as there is for resident sellers.
  • Surcharge on these gains is capped at 15%, so the effective long-term rate tops out at 14.95%.
  • A lower or nil deduction certificate lets the buyer withhold only what you're likely to owe. Apply early; it takes time.
