The key dates

  • 28 March 2026 — Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated phase one.
  • 15 June 2026 — commercial operations began with an IndiGo flight from Lucknow; Akasa Air began services soon after.
  • International flights — airport officials said in mid-2026 they expected international operations to begin by the end of the year. Check current schedules with airlines before planning around them.

What phase one is

One runway and one passenger terminal designed for about 12 million passengers a year. The airport sits in Gautam Buddha Nagar district on the Yamuna Expressway, in the area administered by YEIDA. For families in western UP — Aligarh, Mathura, Bulandshahr, Agra and the Noida–Greater Noida belt — it can be a closer alternative to Delhi's IGI airport, depending on routes and fares.

Planning a trip

  • Compare total door-to-door time and cost, not just the flight: a cheaper fare into Delhi can still lose to Jewar if home is near the expressway.
  • Domestic connections from Jewar to Lucknow and other cities make it useful for onward trips inside UP.
  • OCI cardholders travel visa-free; other foreign passport holders need a visa or e-visa.

About the property pitches

Every major airport triggers a wave of “buy now near the airport” marketing. YEIDA's own plans include industrial areas, a film city and logistics parks, and infrastructure can support values over many years. But much of the story is already in the price, and some offers target NRIs with land they aren't allowed to buy. Before you pay anything:

  • Confirm the land isn't agricultural — NRIs and OCIs cannot buy agricultural land.
  • Check the project on UP RERA and the land on UP Bhulekh.
  • Prefer authority allotments and registered projects; treat “assured returns” as a warning sign.
