Standards

Editorial policy

Sources first

We prefer primary sources: Acts and rules, government portals and press releases, regulator data, court and parliamentary records, and peer-reviewed or university-press history. Where we rely on reputable reporting, we say so and link to it. Every guide lists its sources.

Dated and reviewed

Every guide shows the date it was last reviewed. We re-check guides when a rule they depend on changes, and at least once a year.

Independent of money

Advertisers, sponsors and professional partners never see drafts, never change calculator results and never decide what we recommend. Sponsored content is always labelled.

How we use software and AI

We use software tools, including AI, to help research and draft. Every figure, rule and date is checked against the cited sources before publishing, and calculators are tested against worked examples.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it quickly and note significant corrections on the page. Report an error through our contact page.

Automated content

Exchange rates, news headlines and the video library refresh automatically each day from public sources. Headlines link to their publishers and are not edited by us.
