The words you'll meet

TermWhat it means
KhatauniThe record of rights for a village: who holds which land, in what share, under what class of tenure.
Khata numberThe account number grouping all land held by the same holder or set of co-holders in a village.
Khasra / gata numberThe number of an individual plot on the village map.
Dakhil-kharijMutation: entering the new holder's name (dakhil) and removing the old one (kharij).
Tehsil / lekhpalThe revenue sub-division office and the village-level revenue official who reports on mutations.

Reading your khatauni from abroad

The state's UP Bhulekh portal lets anyone view the current khatauni. You choose the district, tehsil and village, then search by khata number, khasra number or name. The online copy is for information; for court or a sale you'll usually need a certified copy from the tehsil or an authorised service centre.

Check four things every time: the names and their shares, the area against your deed, any note of a stay order or pending case, and the land class — whether it is recorded as agricultural.

Save a dated PDF or screenshot each time you check. A series of saved records showing your family's name is useful evidence if an entry is ever changed.

Mutation after a sale

A registered sale deed transfers ownership, but the revenue record changes only through mutation. After the registry, the purchaser applies for dakhil-kharij; the case is reported on by the lekhpal, notice is given so objections can be raised, and the tehsildar passes an order. Only then does the khatauni show the new name.

Mutation after a death

This is where NRI families most often get stuck, because the heirs live in three countries and nobody wants to fly in. In outline:

  1. Get the death certificate of the recorded holder.
  2. Prove who the heirs are: the family (parivar) register extract held locally, a legal-heir or succession certificate, or a registered will or probate.
  3. Apply to the tehsil for mutation in favour of the heirs, with identity proof for each. Heirs abroad can act through a specific power of attorney.
  4. Respond to any objection; if a relative disputes the heirship, the matter can become a revenue case.
  5. Check the new khatauni carefully when the order is passed. Spelling, shares and missing heirs are common errors and are far easier to fix now than later.
Why it's urgent: land that stays in a dead person's name for years is easier to grab, harder to sell and more likely to be split by disputes between branches of the family.

When the record is wrong

Errors in name, share or area can be corrected through an application to the revenue authorities with supporting documents. Entries made fraudulently — for example on the basis of a forged document or a false death report — usually need a revenue case and a police complaint together. NRIs can also lodge grievances with the UP NRI Department.
