# NRIup.com — Research log & idea rationale

Researched 26 November 2026. Every site below was opened and read directly; “What it does” records what the page actually showed.

## The idea: nriUP — “Uttar Pradesh, handled from anywhere”

**Position:** the independent, practical hub for everyone outside UP with business inside it — property, papers, money, family, roots and visits — monetised mainly through consented, district-matched leads to vetted professionals, with AdSense as the floor.

**Why UP + NRI, not generic NRI:** generic NRI head terms are owned by banks, fintechs and national media. UP-specific long-tail (khatauni, mutation, UP stamp duty, Jewar, Girmitiya origins) is under-served: the state's own NRI portal is a government site with no tools and empty news/events at the time of our visit. Meanwhile the audience is large and specific:
- UP workers received **4,25,851** emigration clearances from 1 Jan 2021 to 19 Nov 2024 — the most of any state, ahead of Bihar (2,17,335) (Rajya Sabha reply via data.gov.in).
- About **80%** of Fiji's North Indian indentured emigrants from the 1880s to 1916 came from UP (Brij V. Lal, *Girmitiyas*, ANU Press); over a million Indians sailed under indenture overall.
- India received **$135.46 billion** in remittances in FY25 (RBI).
- Property is the highest-intent pain point: land left behind, unfinished mutation, encroachment, and the TDS cash trap when NRIs sell (12.5% + surcharge + cess on the full price).

**Revenue model (assumptions to validate, month 12):**
| Stream | Assumption | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| AdSense | 60k sessions × 2 pages, blended RPM $3–7 (mixed India / US-UK-CA-Gulf traffic) | $360–840 |
| Partner leads | 1.2% of sessions submit = 720 leads; 40% opt in to partner share = 288; $12–20 per lead | $3,450–5,750 |
| Sponsorships | 2–4 tool/newsletter/contest sponsors | $300–900 |
| Supporters | 20–40 recurring | $150–400 |

Leads are the business; ads are the floor. The binding constraint is partner supply, so launch partner coverage in five districts first.
