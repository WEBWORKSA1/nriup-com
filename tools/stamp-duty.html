UP stamp duty & registration calculator

The same rates apply to NRIs and residents. What changes the bill is who the buyer is and whether the circle rate is higher than your price.

Circle rates are set by each district's collector and listed on IGRS UP. If yours is higher than the deal value, duty is charged on the circle value.
