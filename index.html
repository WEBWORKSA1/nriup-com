Frequently asked

Can an NRI buy property in Uttar Pradesh?

Yes. NRIs and OCI cardholders can buy residential and commercial property without RBI approval. They cannot buy agricultural land, plantations or farmhouses, though they can inherit them. See our buying guide.

How do I check my land record (khatauni) from abroad?

The UP Bhulekh portal shows the current khatauni online by district, tehsil and village. A certified copy needs the tehsil or a Jan Seva Kendra. Our land-records guide walks you through it.

Is nriUP a government website?

No. nriUP is independent. For official services we link to the government portals — for example the UP NRI Department's own site at nri.up.gov.in.
