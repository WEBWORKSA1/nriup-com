About

Built for people who love UP from far away

nriUP is an independent website for anyone with property, family, roots or plans in Uttar Pradesh who lives outside it.

Why it exists

Uttar Pradesh is India's most populous state and has one of its largest diasporas: professionals in North America, Europe and Australia; workers across the Gulf; and the descendants of indentured labourers in Fiji, Mauritius, the Caribbean and South Africa. Yet the practical information they need is scattered across dozens of government portals, written for residents, and rarely explained for someone dealing with it from abroad. nriUP puts it in one place, in plain English, with tools that do the arithmetic.

What we do

  • Guides written from primary sources, dated and reviewed.
  • Free tools for stamp duty, TDS, residential status, accounts, transfers and checklists.
  • Live data: daily exchange rates, news headlines and videos, refreshed automatically.
  • Free case reviews, with optional introductions to vetted local professionals.
  • Community: the Mera UP contest, the UP Letter and a supporters' programme.

How we make money

Display advertising served by Google AdSense; sponsorships that are always labelled; fees from professional partners when a reader explicitly asks to be introduced; and voluntary support from readers. None of these changes what our tools calculate or what our guides conclude. Read our editorial policy.

What we are not

We are not a government body, a bank, a law firm or a tax practice, and we are not affiliated with the Government of Uttar Pradesh or its NRI Department. Official services are linked to their official portals.
