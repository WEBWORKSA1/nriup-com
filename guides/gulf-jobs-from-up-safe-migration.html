The numbers

From 1 January 2021 to 19 November 2024, workers from Uttar Pradesh received 4,25,851 emigration clearances — the most of any state, ahead of Bihar (2,17,335) and West Bengal (98,822), according to a reply in the Rajya Sabha. Most go to the Gulf.

ECR or ECNR?

Your passport is either ECR (Emigration Check Required) or ECNR. ECR holders going to work in any of the 18 notified countries — including the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain and Malaysia — need emigration clearance through eMigrate before they fly. Clearance is how the government knows where you are and who employed you.

Checking the agent

  • Use only a recruiting agent whose licence you can find on eMigrate. Ask for the licence number and check it yourself.
  • Be suspicious of anyone who says clearance “isn't needed” or offers a visit visa for a work job.
  • Pay only what the rules allow, against a written receipt. Never pay a “sub-agent” in cash.

Before you sign

  • Get the contract in a language you understand: wage, hours, overtime, food, housing, leave, return ticket.
  • Make sure the job and employer named in the contract match the visa.
  • Buy the Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana policy, the mandatory insurance for ECR workers.
  • Leave copies of your passport, visa, contract and the agent's details with family.

If things go wrong

  1. Contact the Indian embassy or consulate. Gulf missions run helplines and can help with unpaid wages, passport confiscation and emergencies.
  2. Your family in UP can file a grievance on the MEA's MADAD portal.
  3. Missions can support workers in distress through the Indian Community Welfare Fund.
  4. The UP NRI Department also accepts grievances from UP families.
Never surrender your passport to an employer to “keep safe.” In most Gulf countries that practice is itself unlawful.
