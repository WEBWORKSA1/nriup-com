Am I still an NRI for tax?

Your tax status is decided afresh every year by days in India, your Indian income and your citizenship — not by your passport or visa.

Why this matters

Status decides what India can tax. An NRI pays Indian tax only on income earned or received in India. A resident and ordinarily resident person is taxed on worldwide income. The middle status, RNOR, is a valuable buffer for people moving back. Gulf-based NRIs should look closely at the deemed-residency rule, because living in a country with no income tax can switch it on.
