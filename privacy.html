Legal

Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026

Who we are

NRIup.com (“nriUP”, “we”) is an independent information website. You can reach us through our contact page.

What we collect

  • What you send us through forms: name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, country, district, the details of your request, and your consent choices.
  • Technical data your browser sends when pages load: IP address, browser type and pages visited, processed by our hosting provider and by Google for advertising.
  • Local storage in your own browser for your theme, cookie choice, checklist ticks and the page you arrived from. This stays on your device.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and handle your request.
  • To send the UP Letter if you subscribe (unsubscribe anytime).
  • To share your request with up to two professionals in your district only if you tick the box asking us to.
  • To show and measure advertising, as described below.
  • To prevent spam and abuse.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Advertising is provided by Google AdSense. Videos load from YouTube only when you press play. Headlines link to their publishers. We do not sell personal data.

Advertising and cookies

Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through a certified consent message. See our Cookie Policy.

How long we keep it

Case and contact messages: up to 24 months after our last exchange. Newsletter: until you unsubscribe. Contest entries: up to 24 months after the season ends. Job and partner applications: up to 12 months.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the EU and UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and US state privacy laws such as the CCPA — you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, withdraw consent, and object to or restrict processing. Send a privacy request through our contact page. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

nriUP is not directed at anyone under 18, and contests are for adults only.

Security

The site runs over HTTPS, forms include spam protection, and our inbox address is not published on the site. No method of transmission is perfectly secure.

Changes

We will post any changes here with a new effective date.
