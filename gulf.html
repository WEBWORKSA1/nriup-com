Workers abroad

Work abroad safely — and get help fast

Between January 2021 and November 2024, workers from Uttar Pradesh received 4,25,851 emigration clearances, the highest of any state and nearly double second-placed Bihar. This hub is for them and their families.

ECR, ECNR and eMigrate in one minute

Indian passports are either ECR (Emigration Check Required) or ECNR. If your passport is ECR and you're going to work in one of the 18 notified ECR countries — which include the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain and Malaysia — you need emigration clearance through the government's eMigrate system. eMigrate also lists licensed recruiting agents and registered foreign employers.

Before you pay anyone

  • Check the recruiting agent's licence on eMigrate. Unlicensed “sub-agents” in your town are where most frauds start.
  • Get the job offer and contract in writing, in a language you understand, with salary, hours, food and housing spelled out.
  • Never travel on a visit visa for a job you were promised — you'll have no legal protection.
  • Take the Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana insurance policy required for ECR workers.
  • Leave copies of everything with your family, and never hand your passport to an employer or agent to keep.

If something goes wrong abroad

Contact the Indian embassy or consulate in that country first; most Gulf missions run worker helplines and shelters. Families in UP can file a grievance on the Ministry of External Affairs' MADAD portal, which covers consular problems such as unpaid wages, passport confiscation and deaths abroad. Missions can use the Indian Community Welfare Fund to help workers in distress.

Sending money home from the Gulf

Exchange houses and apps can differ by more than you think on the same day. Compare two quotes against the mid-market rate before every transfer using our true-cost calculator, and send into an NRE account if you're saving.

Watch for the deemed-residency trap. If you're an Indian citizen in a country with no income tax and your Indian income goes above ₹15 lakh, India may treat you as resident. Check here.

From the UP government

The UP NRI Department runs registrations for overseas job seekers and employers and accepts grievances from UP families. Guides: safe migration from UP · safe-migration checklist.
